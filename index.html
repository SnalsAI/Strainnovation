Our Technologies

01

Shape Memory Polymer Composites

Intelligent Space Mechanisms

Revolutionary SMPC technology enabling deployable structures, solar panel hinges, and adaptive mechanisms for satellites. Our hinge-HDRM system provides reliable actuation in extreme space environments.

  • Lightweight deployment systems
  • Temperature-responsive actuation
  • Low power consumption
  • Radiation-resistant formulations
-150°C to +150°C Operating Range
100N Actuation Force
>10,000 Fatigue Cycles
02

Sustainable Composite Recycling

Circular Economy Solutions

Innovative pyrolysis and chemical processes for recovering valuable materials from aerospace composites and printed circuit boards. Closing the loop in advanced manufacturing.

  • Carbon fiber recovery (>95% quality retention)
  • Precious metal extraction from PCBs
  • Energy-efficient processes
  • Zero-waste approach
